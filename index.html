About Liam

Support that starts with listening.

I’m Liam Jilla, an ACA Level 2 Registered Counsellor with experience in the counselling profession since 2021.

I believe everyone deserves a space where they feel heard, understood and supported without fear of judgement. My approach is friendly, approachable and centred on building a supportive counselling relationship.

I work collaboratively with clients to explore their experiences, navigate challenges and develop practical strategies that fit their individual needs and goals.

Through telehealth, counselling can be accessed from the comfort and privacy of your own environment, wherever you are in Australia (subject to service suitability and availability).

A note about privacy
Please keep your enquiry brief and avoid including sensitive personal or health information in this form. Further information about privacy and consent can be provided before counselling begins.

This form will open your email app with your enquiry ready to send. It does not submit or store information on this website.

Important support information
This counselling service is not an emergency or crisis service and is not monitored continuously. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000 in Australia. For urgent mental health support, contact Lifeline on 13 11 14 or attend the nearest emergency department. If you are unsure whether this service is suitable for an immediate safety concern, please seek urgent professional assistance.

Counselling is not a substitute for emergency care, medical assessment or other specialist services. The suitability of counselling will be discussed as part of the initial enquiry and assessment process.
